Meads Bay or Shoal Bay? Planning a Private Massage Day in Anguilla

Two famous beaches, two different days. Build your massage around where you actually stay.

← Anguilla Wellness Journal · November 3, 2026

Quick answer: Meads Bay is a convenient base for West End resort and villa days. Shoal Bay East is a strong choice for a classic beach-focused day on the island's eastern side. For mobile massage, the best location is not necessarily the prettiest beach—it is the private room, suite or approved setting where your therapist can work comfortably.

Meads Bay: resort comfort and West End dining

Meads Bay is associated with beachfront accommodation and restaurants. It makes sense for guests who want a long beach walk, a quiet lunch and an appointment back at the villa before dinner. The local Anguilla visitor guide identifies the West End and its bays as a significant accommodation and dining district.

If you are staying near the West End, start with our Meads Bay massage area page and share the exact property, not just the beach name.

Shoal Bay East: a beach-first itinerary

Shoal Bay East attracts travelers who want to spend much of the day close to the sand. A mobile appointment can follow a swim or a lunch stop, ideally back at the villa or in an approved indoor location where the therapist has enough space. Find out more about our Shoal Bay coverage.

Do you need a massage table on the sand?

Not necessarily. The quietest and most reliable arrangement is often a suitable private room. A beach massage is a specific setup that depends on weather, privacy, access and permission from the property—not something guaranteed simply because you booked accommodation near the water. See the Anguilla beach massage page for that separate request.

Choose the treatment, not just the setting

For a slow holiday afternoon, Swedish massage is usually the relaxation-first option. If you prefer firmer work on ordinary muscle tension, consider deep tissue. For an anniversary or honeymoon, couples massage brings two treatments into one private experience when suitable space and therapists are available.

A simple beach-to-massage day

  1. Spend the morning and early afternoon on your chosen beach.
  2. Return to your accommodation, rinse off sunscreen and allow time to cool down.
  3. Use a shaded, level private room or covered terrace with an indoor backup.
  4. Leave a gap between the treatment and any dinner reservations or onward transport.

What the concierge needs to know

Send the exact villa or hotel name, number of guests, preferred day, timing, service and whether an outside therapist is permitted on site. The request is subject to therapist and property confirmation.

FAQs

Can a therapist come to an Anguilla hotel?

Potentially, where the hotel permits external wellness providers. Check hotel policy first and include the hotel name in your request.

Are Meads Bay and Shoal Bay interchangeable when booking?

No. They are different parts of Anguilla. The correct location is necessary for travel planning and appointment confirmation.
